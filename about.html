About Clean City

We are committed to creating cleaner, healthier communities through recycling, education and local action.

Our Way: The Clean City Difference

Clean City is a community-driven initiative that promotes sustainable practices and environmental responsibility. We work with local residents, businesses and organisations to reduce waste, increase recycling and create cleaner, healthier communities. Our programmes include educational workshops, community clean-up events and partnerships with local businesses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote sustainable practices and environmental responsibility in our community. We strive to reduce waste, increase recycling and create a cleaner, healthier environment for all residents. Together we can make a positive impact on our neighbourhoods and the planet.

See our services

Our Values

We value sustainability, community engagement and environmental responsibility. We believe that by working together we can create a cleaner, healthier environment for all residents, and our programmes are designed to turn those values into action.
