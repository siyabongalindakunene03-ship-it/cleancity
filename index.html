Our Programmes

We offer a range of programmes to keep our city clean and beautiful, from everyday waste collection to community action and education.

Waste Management

Weekly household pick-ups, business collections and a free drop-off point in Newtown keep recyclable material out of landfill.

Community Clean-Up

Regular street and park clean-ups bring neighbours together to clear litter and care for shared public spaces.
