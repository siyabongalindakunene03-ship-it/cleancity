Enquiries about Clean City

We are committed to creating cleaner, healthier communities through recycling, education and local action. If you have any questions or would like to learn more about our programs and services, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. Our team is always happy to help and provide information on how you can get involved in making a positive impact on our environment.

Other Ways to Get Involved

Not ready to send a message? Here are a few other ways you can support Clean City's work in your community.

Frequently Asked Questions

A few quick answers before you send us a message.

How do I sign up to volunteer?

Select "Volunteering" from the enquiry type dropdown above and tell us a bit about yourself — our Community Outreach team will follow up with upcoming events near you.

What items can I donate for recycling?

We accept clean paper, plastic, glass, and metal. Check our Services page for the full list of what we collect and how to prepare it.

How quickly will I hear back?

Our team typically responds within 2 business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly using the number above.

Can my business partner with Clean City?

Yes — select "Partnership" in the form above and share a few details about your organisation. We'll be in touch to discuss how we can work together.
