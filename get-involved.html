How Volunteering Works

Getting started takes three simple steps.

1. Pick an event

Choose a clean-up, drive or workshop from the list above that suits your area and your schedule.

2. Tell us you are coming

Use the volunteer button on the event or send an enquiry so we can reserve your places.

3. Turn up and help

Arrive in closed shoes with a bottle of water. We provide gloves, bags and refreshments.
